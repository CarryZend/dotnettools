Why DotNetKit?

Most online developer tool sites are cluttered with ads, slow to load, and don't understand the .NET ecosystem. DotNetKit is different: every tool is built by a developer with 10 years of C# experience, designed specifically for the .NET workflow. Whether you need to test a regex with System.Text.RegularExpressions named groups, generate C# classes from a JSON payload, or validate your appsettings.json, these tools understand .NET conventions natively.

Privacy First

All tools run entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device — no server-side processing, no tracking, no analytics on your input. This is especially important when working with sensitive configuration files or production data snippets.
